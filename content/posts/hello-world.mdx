## Why frontmatter matters

Everything above the `---` is frontmatter. `title`, `description` and `date`
are required; `tags` and `draft` are optional. The listing page sorts by
`date`, newest first.

## What a good post looks like

Pick one thing you actually did and explain the decision, not just the steps:

- What was the problem?
- What did you try that did not work?
- What did you choose in the end, and what did it cost?

That last question is the one most write-ups skip, and the one interviewers
care about.
